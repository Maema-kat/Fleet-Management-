#Project Vision 
To provide organizations with a centralized ,secure and user-friendly management platform that improves operational efficiency , enhances vehicle and driver oversight while reducing adminstrattive efforts

#Objectives
Centralize fleet information, improve vehicle utilization ,record fuel usage ,track maintenance ,improve reporting 

#Target Users 
fleet manager, driver, mechanic 

#Expected Benefits 
For business - Lower administrative overhead ,better compliance ,reduced maintenance surprises 
For users - Easier Workflows ,faster information access 

#Success Criteria 
Fleet managers can manage vehicles ,drivers, fuels and maintenance from one application
application is responsive and easy to use
