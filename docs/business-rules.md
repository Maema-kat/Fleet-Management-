#Business Rules 

#Driver rules - driver must have a valid and unique license number ,a driver may only have one active vehicle assignment at a time, a suspended driver cannot request or operate vehicle ,a driver may only request a vehicle at least 24 hours before intended trip ,a driver cannot edit intended trip 

#Vehicle rules- a vehicle may only be assigned to one driver at a time ,a vehicle under maintenance cannot be assigned, each vehicle will have a status  e.g available, assigned ,archived, under maintenance

#Vehicle request rules - every vehicle request must include destination ,purpose of travel, departure date and return date, a vehicle request must be approved before vehicle assigned , rejected requests must include rejection reason ,only fleet managers may approve or reject requests 

#Trip Rules - a trip cannot end before it starts ,ending odometer must be greater or equal to the starting odometer , drivers must submit the ending odometer reading when completing a trip 

#Fuel Rules -fuel costs must be greater than zero, fuel odometer reading cannot be lower than vehicle's latest recorded odometer 

#Maintenance Rules-maintenance records must include service date ,type brief description and cost
